Current Projects carried out at DNS Lab include:

Digital Agriculture AI for smart digital Agriculture systems

This project focuses on utilizing AI models for the improvement of digital agriculture systems, by reducing loss of crops and increasing yield. Currently we focus on systems for the early identification of plant diseases in field through plant leaf disease identification DL computer vision models.

digital-agriculture
Fed Med (ITRC) Federated Learning AI for the medical field.

Privacy-preserving distributed learning framework. Research on a privacy-preserving distributed learning framework based on federated learning.

fed-med
DNS-Based DID Resolution (KISA) Decentralized Identifier Resolution basde on DNS

Research on DNS-based resolution to support CRUD operations across diverse DID methods without relying on the centralized Universal Resolver.

did
GITRC – Mobility AI AI for smart digital Agriculture systems

Mobility intelligence for networks using AI. Research on AI-driven vehicle traffic flow optimization and inter-vehicle communication protocols.

gitrc
SLAM Simultaneous Localization and Mapping indoor system

Point cloud processing, localization, and map generation. Research on path planning using 2D and 3D LiDAR and point cloud data.

slam
Glocal LAB – AI + Military AI-assisted agent for the military

Planned research on improving radar object detection accuracy and exploring AI adoption in military applications. Status: Preparatory stage; full-scale research has not yet started.

glocal-lab
AICONV – AI AI for healthcare, energy & monitoring systems

AI for healthcare, energy & monitoring systems.

aiconv
MARL - BIMS Smart Building Support with MARL based Energy Saving

Smart Building Support with MARL based Energy Saving. Research on optimizing building energy consumption using multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL).

marl
