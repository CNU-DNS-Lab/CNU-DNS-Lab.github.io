I am a master’s student in Artificial Intelligence Convergence at Chonnam National University (DNSLAB, advisor: Prof. Kyungbaek Kim). My primary research identity is AI-based security — intrusion detection, security machine learning, and policy systems for cloud and distributed environments. I also have substantial experience in decentralized identity (DID) — method security, interoperability, and KISA-related work — which complements my interest in trust and verification in distributed systems. Other collaborations (networked AI, applied sensing) appear under Projects and Publications. Research Interests:

  • AI-based intrusion detection
  • Security machine learning
  • Explainable policy systems
  • Hybrid-cloud security
  • Federated decision fusion
  • Decentralized identity (DID)
