3 papers by DNS Lab students have been accepted to and will be presented at this years International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Information and Communication (ICAIIC) in Tokyo, Japan from Feb. 24 to 27. 
The three papers are:
- [Evaluating Knowledge Distillation for Plant Leaf Disease Classification (DJ Richter, MI Bappi, K Kim)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICAIIC68212.2026.11454411)
- [Multi-Scale Convolutional Reconstruction Defense Against Adversarial and Real-World Corruptions in Plant Disease Recognition (MDI Bappi, U Shakhakarmi, J Shin, K Kim)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICAIIC68212.2026.11454422)
- [Federated Speech Encoder Fine-Tuning for Multilingual Cross Site Parkinson's Speech Classification: Performance Fairness Analysis (S Kolekar, J Shin, H Nam, K Kim)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICAIIC68212.2026.11454181)
